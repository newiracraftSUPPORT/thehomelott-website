Reporting a wrong result

If we have published a result that does not match the organiser’s draw sheet, we want to correct it as a priority. Please include:

  • the lottery name;
  • the draw date;
  • the drawn number or winning numbers as published by the organiser;
  • what our page showed, and a link to it;
  • a link or screenshot of the organiser’s own published result, if you have one.

That is enough for us to check the source and correct the record. We note corrections rather than silently overwriting them, so the change is traceable.

You can also report an issue from inside the app: open the relevant ticket or lottery and choose Report a problem. In this build the report is stored locally rather than sent, so please use email until the sending service is connected.

Before you write

Your tickets are in your own browser. We never receive them, so please do not send us ticket numbers, scans of tickets, or screenshots showing your numbers unless the report genuinely requires it — and redact anything else visible in the image.

We cannot look up, retrieve, or restore your tickets. We have no copy to look up. If you have lost access to your data, the fix is to re-enter it from your own records or any export you made.

Other enquiries

  • A lottery is missing from the catalogue — tell us the organiser’s name and, ideally, a link to their official page. We add lotteries on a sourcing basis, not on request alone, so we need somewhere verifiable to read from.
  • Requesting a correction to a lottery’s details — include the organiser’s own published details and we will verify against them.
  • Reusing our data or content — write to legal@thehomelott.com. Results carry a source, so please keep the attribution and the verification label intact.
  • Affiliate questions — see the affiliate disclosure first; it answers most of them.
  • Media or research enquiries — email hello@thehomelott.com with your outlet and deadline.

Social media

We do not operate social media accounts, and we are not on X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or any gambling-affiliate network. If you find an account claiming to be Home Lotts, it is not us — please report it to the platform.

We keep our conversations here, in email, where we can be precise and accountable.
